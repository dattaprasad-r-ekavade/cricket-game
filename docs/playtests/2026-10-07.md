# Playtest 1 — 7 October 2026

- **Build:** Release, Step 49 working tree (uncommitted ball-follow camera), default settings: 1 over per innings, CPU Standard.
- **Tester:** project owner, keyboard.
- **Scope:** first human play session of the match build.

## Findings

| # | Problem | Impact | Plan task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The controls can't be learned. About 25 keys, shown as one dense text block on the HUD; no mental model of what to press when. | Blocking | A4a |
| 2 | The batting mechanic isn't understood: when to press, how the shots differ, why a shot hit or missed. | Blocking | A4b |
| 3 | No feedback about the delivery that was bowled (pace, line, length, pitch point) or about the shot (timing, contact quality). | Blocking | A4c |

## Other observations

- The window title still reads "Super Cricket — One Over" although the build plays a two-innings match.

## Next

Implement A4a–A4c from `plan.md`, then repeat the playtest with the same build settings and at least one person who has never seen the game.
