## Response in Step 51

The default batting broadcast now frames the pitch at 28 m; the behind-striker view is 21 m and the bowler-end view is 24 m. The game selects the camera for the player role when a delivery begins and switches to ball-follow after bat contact. A persistent last-delivery card reports ball speed, measured pitch length and line, shot/contact quality, runs or wicket, and bowling target error when the player bowled. The Release review captures both batting and bowling versions of that card.

This is code and capture validation, not a human retest of Step 51. Keyboard readability remains to be confirmed, and GamePad play has not been tested.

## Response in Step 52

The batting result card now compares the player's shot-button press against the analyzer-measured best-contact delay for the selected stock delivery and shot. It labels input within 75 ms of that target `PERFECT`; otherwise it reports `EARLY` or `LATE` with the offset in milliseconds. The checked calibration targets cover standard, wide, no-ball, and yorker deliveries, and use the stock delivery identity when CPU bowling adds a variation name. The batting feedback capture visibly shows the new timing label; the bowling result card continues to show target and landing error.

Release build, gameplay checks, calibration-drift checks, 20 live-match trace replays, and screenshot captures pass. This is still code/capture validation: keyboard readability and the usefulness of the timing label have not yet been confirmed by the owner, and GamePad play remains untested.
