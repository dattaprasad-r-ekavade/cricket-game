This is code and capture validation, not a human retest of Step 51. Keyboard readability remains to be confirmed, and GamePad play has not been tested.

## Response in Step 52

The batting result card now compares the player's shot-button press against the analyzer-measured best-contact delay for the selected stock delivery and shot. The checked calibration targets cover standard, wide, no-ball, and yorker deliveries, and use the stock delivery identity when CPU bowling adds a variation name. The batting feedback capture visibly shows the new timing label; the bowling result card continues to show target and landing error.

Release build, gameplay checks, calibration-drift checks, 20 live-match trace replays, and screenshot captures pass. This is still code/capture validation: keyboard readability and the usefulness of the timing label have not yet been confirmed by the owner, and GamePad play remains untested.

### Code review observations

- The compact HUD still renders score, player names, last event, and controls as one flat text block. Its batting hint says `Space: ground / defend`, but the implementation selects defence at neutral aim and a drive after a directional aim; the live aim-lane status helper is not drawn.
- The behind-striker camera is already the default batting preset, but its yaw is offset by about 13 degrees, putting the view left of the pitch axis rather than directly behind the batter.
- Keyboard shot input does switch the striker to the authored block/drive/loft clip, but the current shot clip loops and the player-facing HUD does not identify the active animation or the exact input that selected a shot. Visual response needs a clearer action and human retest.

## Response in Step 111

The flat text overlay is now split into a score panel and a phase-specific action panel with keycaps. The batting panel says explicitly that centre + Space/A defends, while a selected direction + Space/A drives; Shift/Y lofts, and the lane meter shows the selected direction. Live batting feedback and the persistent result card identify the shot and the input used. The behind-striker camera now looks straight down the pitch axis, and shot clips play once before returning to stance; the action panel reports the current swing state.

The 1440x900 batting HUD and FOUR-feedback Release captures were inspected. The focused animation, camera, HUD, and gameplay checks, all 358 tests, and `tools/review.ps1` pass. This verifies code paths and screenshots; it does not establish that the animation feels responsive in play. A keyboard retest is still needed for shot controls, animation response, and batting/bowling feedback. GamePad remains untested.

### Response in Step 112

The control research found a secondary PC guide listing arrows for direction; `S` for defence; `S+D` for a forward stroke; `W+D` for a hit behind; `Shift+S` for a six attempt; and `D`/`A` for running/run back. The installed readme warns some keyboards may drop simultaneous keys and does not include those PC bindings. Step 112 records ordered keydown events for 220 ms, starts the block animation immediately on `S`, and switches to a front-foot stroke if `D` follows. `W+D` selects a new back-foot hit-behind animation, and Shift selects a lofted attempt. The timing and contact systems stay active; GamePad controls are unchanged.

The automated verification passes; the new Release still needs the owner's keyboard retest for key rollover, buffer feel, direction, and animation response. GamePad play remains untested.
